# Privacy Policy

**Nexus** is an autonomous agent designed for local-first intelligence.

*   **Data Sovereignty:** Your code, your history, your memory, and your API keys stay on your local device.
*   **Zero-Telemetry:** Nexus does not track usage or collect analytics. 
*   **Transient Memory:** The vector-mesh uses local SQLite; it is never replicated to a cloud server unless you explicitly configure a sync endpoint.

If you have questions about how your data is handled, please reach out to [support@alpha1studio.com].
